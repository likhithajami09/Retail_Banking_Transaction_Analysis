# Data Quality Notes

1. `close_date` was blank for the 497 open accounts; loaded through a staging table using `NULLIF()`.
2. All 7 files passed key checks: no duplicate primary keys and no orphan foreign keys.
3. 13 of 40 branches share a name (27 unique names), so branch analysis groups by `branch_id`, not `branch_name`.
4. `paid_amount` exceeds `scheduled_amount` in 308 of 1,073 paid/late payments, so paid-vs-scheduled ratios were not used; `status` and `days_late` were used instead.
5. Transaction status is split almost equally (33.8% / 33.7% / 32.6%), and Fee/Interest amounts are as large as debits. `balance_after` looks inconsistent, so treat it with caution.
6. 98.7% of transactions are from 2022-2024; there are none in Jan 2025.
7. Customer segments do not differ meaningfully in income or credit score.

# Final Conclusions and Recommendations

| Area | Summary |
|---|---|
| Customers | 500 customers, 75% active, 56 cities; only 28.6% fully KYC-verified; 241 active customers have Partial/Pending KYC |
| Accounts | 700 accounts holding $10.2M, concentrated in IRA, CD and Money Market; 29% closed; branch workload varies about 6x |
| Transactions | 5,000 transactions, growing yearly, led by Online/Mobile, but only 33.8% complete |
| Loans | 56.3% Defaulted or In Arrears, holding 73.3% of the $53.4M outstanding; 31 matured loans still owe $8.0M |
| Cards | 93.8% inactive, 74.2% expired; utilization healthy at 41.8%; 74 customers hold no product |

## Top recommendations

1. Launch collections and restructuring for Defaulted/In Arrears loans (start with the 31 matured loans owing $8.0M) and tighten Auto, Home and Business underwriting.
2. Fix transaction processing: two thirds of transactions fail or stay pending in every channel.
3. Complete KYC for the 241 active Partial/Pending customers.
4. Push Auto Debit for loan repayments (19.6% late rate vs 28.3% for cash) and add pre-due reminders.
5. Reactivate and renew cards (118 unexpired inactive cards first) and cross-sell cards and accounts.
6. Rebalance branch staffing (0.30 to 1.75 accounts per employee) and redefine customer segments.
